**Steps** (the exact tool name and request text are in the version sent to the course team)
1. Log in as `team30c` (or `team31a`) on Suryodaya.
2. List all activities, with the read-only list tool, paging to the end.
3. For each activity, check that the due date is empty or a calendar date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).

**Expected**
Every activity's due date is either empty or a calendar date, as on Keystone, where every activity has a calendar date.

**Actual**
- On Suryodaya, many activities have a short word in the due date instead of a date. The words are all the same length, and some end part-way through a word, so they look like truncated text.
- All of them are open meetings (not done, not cancelled), each linked to a different account and none to a deal. None has a due time or a creator, and all were created on the same day in September 2026.
- The other date fields we read are fine: deal close dates are calendar dates on both systems.

We changed nothing; every request was a read.

**Evidence**
The exact request text, the count and the record ids are sent to the course team. Nothing is attached here, so no platform data is included.

**Impact**
- Seat 30's brief lists each account's open activities and flags the overdue ones, and its next-step rule 2 names the oldest overdue activity. Because these activities belong to accounts, they appear for most Suryodaya deals through the brief's account fallback, so the gap affects most of the Suryodaya brief.
- **What we do now:** the brief shows such an activity as open with no due date, never overdue, and lists it under data issues. It guesses no date. Suite task 7 recounts it the same way, so the brief and the check agree.

**Ask**
Is this intended test data? If not, please fix the due dates of these activities on Suryodaya, or clear them.
